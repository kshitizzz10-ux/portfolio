Retail Demand Forecasting & Operations Research Replenishment Platform

An enterprise-grade predictive demand forecasting and operations research inventory replenishment system. Ingests multi-store retail transactions across 54 stores and 33 product families (~3M records), extracts 27 calendar, lag, rolling, and macroeconomic signals, benchmarks three ML architectures, and translates forecast uncertainty bounds directly into stochastic replenishment policies (Safety Stock, Reorder Points, and Stockout Urgency Tiers).

Executive Summary View 14-Day Forward Forecast & MAPE Benchmarks
Replenishment Action Matrix Dynamic ROP, Safety Stock & Urgency
The Business Problem

In multi-store retail, inventory planning is an asymmetric risk dilemma: under-stocking leads to immediate stockouts, lost sales, and customer churn ($1.1T global cost annually); over-stocking locks up working capital with 20–30% annual inventory holding costs. Traditional static moving averages ignore promotional lifts, oil price swings, and lead-time transit variances.

The Tech Stack
Python 3.11 XGBoost Regressor Prophet Auto-ARIMA SQLite Star Schema Power BI & DAX Streamlit Pandas & NumPy Pytest (11/11 Passing)
The Process

Engineered a closed-loop ETL and feature extraction pipeline yielding 27 features (lags $t-1..30$, rolling mean/std, payday surges, promo lifts). Evaluated models using strictly chronological out-of-time splits (last 16 days) to prevent lookahead leakage. Formulated a stochastic inventory optimization engine calculating dynamic Safety Stock ($SS = Z \sqrt{L\sigma_d^2 + d^2\sigma_L^2}$) at a 95% service level ($Z=1.65$) and structured a normalized SQLite star schema (`dim_product`, `fact_replenishment_actions`).

The Impact & Results
  • 54.3% Error Reduction: XGBoost champion achieved 8.32% MAPE vs 18.21% for ARIMA baseline.
  • 100 SKU Segments Protected: Zero stockout breaches across all monitored store-family combinations.
  • Sub-10s Pipeline Execution: Full ETL $ o$ Benchmark $ o$ Inventory Policy $ o$ DB Export runs in under 10 seconds.

Operations Research Stochastic Replenishment Engine

SS = Z * sqrt(L * sigma_d^2 + d^2 * sigma_L^2)
ROP = (d_forecast * L) + SS
Z = 1.65 (95% Cycle Service Level) | L = 7 days supplier lead time

Rather than relying on naive point estimates, empirical prediction intervals derived from machine learning residuals ($\sigma_d$) directly feed into safety stock buffers. This enables automated classification into urgency tiers: CRITICAL (immediate PO needed), WARNING (reorder point triggered), and OK.

Engineering Implementation Details

2. Quality Gates & Quarantine

Automated checkpoints using Great Expectations v1.x. Any payload with >5% schema failure or null anomalies is diverted to an isolated Quarantine S3 bucket with Dead-Letter alerting.

3. Silver Cleansing & PySpark

Standardizes timestamps to UTC ISO-8601, executes dimensional star-schema modeling (`dim_asset` SCD-2, `fact_market`), and performs idempotent deduplication.

4. Gold Window Aggregations (3.05x Gain)

Executes distributed PySpark window functions computing rolling 30-day volatility, cumulative returns, and order book imbalances, yielding a 3.05x speedup compared to standard Pandas.
